Frequently asked questions

What's included in the free version?

The diagnostic, a daily question, the first 10 practice questions in each subject, 2 tutorials per subject, the English / Reading & Writing flashcard decks, and Mock Exam 1. ACT & SAT Prep Pro unlocks every question (1,400+), tutorial, flashcard deck, and mock exam for both tests.

How do I subscribe, and what does it cost?

Open Settings › Upgrade to Pro in the app. Pro is available monthly or yearly; the exact price in your local currency is shown before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To manage or cancel on iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage subscription inside the app's Settings tab.

I subscribed but the app still shows the free plan.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account used to purchase, then open Settings › Restore Purchases in the app.

Does my progress sync between my devices?

Your subscription works on every device signed in to the same Apple Account (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android). Subscriptions do not carry between Apple and Android. Study progress (scores, streaks, completed tutorials) is stored on each device and does not sync between them in this version.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings › Reset all progress. This clears exam results, practice history, flashcard history, saved essays, and completed tutorials. Your subscription is unaffected.
