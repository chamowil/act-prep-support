Privacy Policy

ACT Prep for iPhone, iPad, and Mac · Last updated 27 August 2026

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Use
The short version: ACT Prep does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not operate any server that receives your data. ACT Prep contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, so it can show your progress and study plan:

  • Your study plan: the name you optionally enter, target score, daily practice goal, and optional test date.
  • Practice history: which questions you answered and whether each was correct.
  • Mock exam results: section scores, composite scores, and dates.
  • Tutorials you have marked as complete, and your daily practice minutes and streak.

This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server we control. It may be included in your own encrypted iCloud or local device backups, which are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether an active subscription exists for the signed-in Apple Account, so it can unlock content. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Children's privacy

ACT Prep is designed for high-school students preparing for a college entrance exam. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it collects nothing from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

5. Data deletion

You can erase everything the app has stored at any time from Settings › Reset Everything inside the app. Deleting the app from your device also removes all locally stored data.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
